OpenPlant is an open-source, privacy-friendly plant companion app for Android.

Track your plants, manage care routines, and never miss a watering again.

Features:
- Plant collection with photos and care history
- Customizable care schedules (watering, fertilizing, misting, pruning, and more)
- Care reminders with notifications
- On-device plant identification using a bundled AI model
- Dark mode and multi-language support (English, German)
- No account required, no data leaves your device

Built with Clean Architecture. AGPL v3 licensed.
